## 🏋️ Featured Project — Modern Fitness Gym Management System

> A full-stack gym management platform built with **Python Flask**, **React.js**, and **SQLite** — covering everything from member attendance to financial reporting.

### What It Does

**Three role-based portals:**

**🔴 Admin**
- Real-time dashboard — active/inactive members, overdue payments at a glance
- Member management — full profiles, admission history, monthly attendance summaries, and payment history
- Automated overdue alerts — sends WhatsApp messages to members with overdue payments
- Email integration for password resets
- Trainer management with commission tracking and receipt printing
- Finance module — per-member financial lookup, monthly receipt generation
- Analytics page for gym performance insights
- Settings panel — configure admission fees, manage accounts

**🔵 Super Admin**
- Full audit log — monitor what receptionists are doing in real time
- Monthly profit & loss statements
- Admin account management
- Today's attendance summary across the gym

**🟢 Member Portal**
- Members can mark and track their own attendance
- Personal attendance history

**⚡ Bonus Features**
- **Turnstile machine integration** — attendance auto-logged via QR code session scanning (hardware-linked)
- **Live attendance pop-ups** powered by Pusher (real-time WebSockets)
- **Birthday pop-ups** — automatic celebration alerts for members on their birthday

### Tech Stack
`Python Flask` · `React.js` · `SQLite` · `Pusher` · `WhatsApp API` · `SMTP Email` · `QR Code / Session Auth`
